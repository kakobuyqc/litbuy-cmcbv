03 / Litbuy shopping guide

From a link to
a better decision.

A useful collection of Litbuy spreadsheet finds includes the information needed to compare items, not just a product image.

01

Start narrow.

Choose one category and a specific need. For a Litbuy shoes spreadsheet, begin with intended use and size. For clothing, begin with fit and measurements.

02

Read the listing.

Open each candidate and check size, color, material, and seller terms. Confirm separately whether your shopping platform accepts that product link.

03

Compare the evidence.

Review available detail photos and measurements. If quality-control photos are available, compare them with the selected variant and seller description.

04

Price the whole order.

Add applicable delivery, service fees, currency costs, shipping, and taxes. Save the date checked so your shortlist does not rely on old figures.

Practical questions about spreadsheet shopping, category links, sizing, and product checks.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and shopping guide. Its browsing links open CAFANS, where you can investigate product listings and build your own shortlist.

How should I check a product link before ordering?

Open the listing, confirm the selected size and color, read the material description, review seller terms, and check the current price. Confirm separately whether your shopping platform accepts that seller link and product.

How do I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Use the specific seller’s size chart. Compare garment measurements with clothing you already own. For shoes, compare foot length with the stated sizing method and confirm whether measurements refer to feet or insoles.

What costs belong in my shopping budget?

Include the item price, applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion charges, and any applicable taxes or duties. An item price alone is not a delivered total.

Are brand images proof of stock or authenticity?

No. Brand images on this guide are editorial references. They do not establish CAFANS inventory, seller authorization, authenticity, platform compatibility, or an affiliation with the brands shown.
